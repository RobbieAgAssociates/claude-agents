# Counter Intelligence (Commander deck)

- Commander: Inspirit, Flagship Vessel
- Product: Edge of Eternities Commander (EOC), released 2025-08-01
- Official decklist: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/edge-of-eternities-commander-decklists
- Status: unmodified precon. Update this file if the user swaps cards.
- Analyzed: 2026-10-10 (Scryfall Oracle text, Commander Spellbook combos)

## 1. Summary

Blue-red-white artifacts and counters. Inspirit is a Spacecraft you "station" by tapping your creatures. Once it reaches 8 counters it flies and makes all your other artifacts hexproof and indestructible. The deck stacks charge and +1/+1 counters on artifacts, then multiplies them with proliferate. It wins with Darksteel Reactor (20 counters = you win), Lux Artillery (10 damage to each opponent) or a big artifact army.

## 2. Key cards and combos

No infinite combos (checked on Commander Spellbook). Its main win lines:

- **Darksteel Reactor + proliferate:** Reactor gains a counter each upkeep, and every proliferate adds another. Reaching 20 wins the game immediately at any moment, even mid-turn. Reactor is indestructible on its own.
- **Deepglow Skate:** doubles every kind of counter on any number of permanents. A Reactor at 10+ goes to 20+, so you win on the spot. It also doubles Lux Artillery's count, Inspirit's charge counters and +1/+1 counters.
- **Lux Artillery:** at your end step, if your artifacts and creatures have 30+ counters in total (charge and +1/+1 both count), it deals 10 damage to each opponent, every turn. It also gives your artifact creature spells sunburst.
- **Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus:** every proliferate happens twice. It also triggers Kilo twice as often, in effect.
- **Kilo, Apogee Mind:** proliferates whenever it becomes tapped, whether by attacking (it has haste), stationing Inspirit, or paying for improvise (Organic Extinction, Kappa Cannoneer, Universal Surveillance). Tapping Kilo to station Inspirit adds 3 charge counters plus a proliferate, and the proliferate resolves first.
- **Inspirit at 8+:** protects your whole artifact board from targeted and "destroy" removal, including board wipes like Wrath of God. It doesn't stop exile, sacrifice, -X/-X or bounce.
- **Other counter engines:** Steel Overseer, Coretapper, Surge Conductor (proliferate whenever a nontoken artifact enters), Resourceful Defense (moves counters off dying permanents), Moxite Refinery, Thrummingbird, Experimental Augury and Tezzeret's Gambit.
- **Big mana:** Astral Cornucopia and Empowered Autogenerator grow with proliferate. Pentad Prism and Everflowing Chalice too.
- **Card draw:** Insight Engine, Uthros Research Craft (draw for each artifact spell once stationed to 3), Jhoira (draw for each artifact or legendary spell) and Universal Surveillance.

## 3. Card balance (my count)

- Lands: 40, including 7 artifact lands.
- Ramp: about 11 (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Pentad Prism, Everflowing Chalice, Astral Cornucopia, Empowered Autogenerator, Solar Array, Crystalline Crawler, Etherium Sculptor, Enthusiastic Mechanaut, Cloud Key).
- Card draw: about 12 (Insight Engine, Uthros Research Craft, Thought Monitor, Universal Surveillance, Pull from Tomorrow, Tezzeret's Gambit, Thirst for Knowledge, Experimental Augury, Jhoira, Etched Oracle, Mindless Automaton, Long-Range Sensor).
- Targeted removal: 6 (Swords to Plowshares, Dispatch, Chaos Warp, Lux Cannon, Angel of the Ruins, Alibou), plus Swan Song.
- Board wipes: 3 (Fumigate, Organic Extinction, which spares your artifact creatures, and Chain Reaction).
- Protection: Inspirit at 8+, Ripples of Potential (phase out), Swan Song, Resourceful Defense, indestructible Bridge lands, and Kappa Cannoneer's ward.

This is the best card draw of the four decks. Removal is on the light side.

## 4. Mana curve

Nonland cards by mana value (X counted as 0): 0: 3 | 1: 5 | 2: 12 | 3: 16 | 4: 12 | 5: 3 | 6: 2 | 7+: 6. Average 3.34. Cost reducers (Etherium Sculptor, Enthusiastic Mechanaut, Cloud Key) and improvise/affinity cards play cheaper than their printed costs.

Colored symbols: blue 26, white 13, red 11. Mostly blue. Inspirit itself needs all three colors.

## 5. Rules to know

- **Station:**
  - Tap another untapped creature you control to put charge counters on the Spacecraft equal to that creature's power.
  - Sorcery speed only.
  - Creatures that just arrived can be tapped for station. Station is the Spacecraft's own ability (CR 702.184a), and summoning sickness only stops a creature's own {T} abilities and attacks (CR 302.6). Confirmed 2026-10-10.
  - Each threshold ("1+", "8+") turns on once the counter count is reached. At 8+ Inspirit becomes an artifact creature.
- **Inspirit's 1+ ability** targets "up to one other artifact" each combat. You choose a +1/+1 counter or two charge counters. Use charge counters on Darksteel Reactor or Lux Cannon.
- **Proliferate:** choose any number of permanents and players that already have counters. Each gets one more of every kind it has. You can skip opponents' permanents.
- **Darksteel Reactor's win** happens as soon as it has 20 counters (a "state trigger"). It doesn't wait for your upkeep. Opponents can respond only by removing counters or stopping the trigger.
- **Lux Artillery** checks at the beginning of your end step. Charge and +1/+1 counters on your artifacts and creatures all count, including Inspirit's.
- **Phasing (Ripples of Potential):** phased-out permanents are treated as if they don't exist until your next untap, so wipes miss them. Counters and Equipment stay with them.
- **Improvise** lets you tap artifacts to pay generic mana. Tapping Kilo that way triggers its proliferate.
- **Phyrexian mana ({U/P}):** you can pay 2 life instead of {U}. It still counts as blue for color identity.
- **Sunburst / converge** count the different colors of mana spent, so pay with colored mana, not Sol Ring.
- **Inspirit isn't a creature below 8 counters,** so it can't attack or deal commander damage until then.

## 6. Power level

No Game Changers, no two-card infinite combos and no mass land denial. It fits Bracket 2 (Core). Darksteel Reactor and Lux Artillery wins can come out of nowhere, so mention them to new players.

## 7. Weak spots and swaps

**Weak spots:**
- Inspirit needs creatures to tap, and only about 25 of the cards are creatures.
- Before Inspirit reaches 8 counters, artifact wipes hurt badly.
- Few counterspells.
- Chain Reaction kills your own artifact creatures.

**Swaps (all stay Bracket 2):**
- **Thrummingbird → Flux Channeler.** Proliferate on every noncreature spell, which is most of this deck, instead of needing combat damage.
- **Mindless Automaton → Inexorable Tide.** Proliferate on every spell you cast.
- **Secluded Steppe → Inventors' Fair.** A land that finds Darksteel Reactor, Lux Artillery or Steel Overseer when needed.
